# Performance requirements of the library application

- PERF-1: With 20 concurrent users, 95% of searches answer in less than 300 ms.
- PERF-2: With 20 concurrent users, 95% of catalogue listings answer in less than 200 ms.
- PERF-3: With 20 concurrent users, less than 1% of reservations fail.
- PERF-4: With 20 concurrent users, the application serves at least 100 requests per second.
